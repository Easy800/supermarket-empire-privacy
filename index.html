Privacy Policy

SuperMart: Idle Craft

Effective date: July 16, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the iPhone and iPad edition of SuperMart: Idle Craft published by Dim & Bright.

Data collection

The app does not collect or transmit personal data, gameplay data, diagnostics, analytics, or advertising data. It does not create user accounts and does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Local game data

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Dim & Bright does not receive this information. Deleting the app may also delete locally stored progress.

Tracking and advertising

The app does not track users across apps or websites, does not access the advertising identifier, and does not display third-party advertising.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If a future app version changes these data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email Support@nixiw.com.
