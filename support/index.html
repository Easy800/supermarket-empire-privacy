Support

SuperMart: Idle Craft

For help with the iPhone or iPad game, email Support@nixiw.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Frequently asked questions

How is my progress saved?

Game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. The game does not require an account or a sign-in.

Can progress be restored after reinstalling or changing devices?

Because progress is stored locally, reinstalling the app or moving to a different device can reset progress. We cannot restore local progress from a server.

The game is not responding or does not start.

Close and reopen the app, then make sure your device is using a current version of iOS or iPadOS. If the issue continues, contact support with the details above.

Does the game show third-party advertising or require an account?

No. This edition does not display third-party advertising and does not require an account.
